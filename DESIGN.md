## Brand & Style

The design system projects a lethal, elite competitive aura tailored for hardcore competitive swordplay, arena combat, and martial gaming esports. The visual identity merges futuristic high-tech tournament interfaces with the ancient discipline of the blade. It targets competitive gamers, esports organizers, broadcast spectators, and hardcore hobbyists who demand high-octane precision, aggressive clarity, and cinematic prestige.

### Aesthetic Foundation
The movement balances **Cyber-Chivalry Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Precision**. Surface depth relies on translucent carbon-tinted panels, razor-sharp metallic rim highlights, and controlled prismatic neon bloom. The interface avoids toy-like neon clutter, channeling an austere, high-stakes combat telemetry console. 

### Visual Tenets
- **The Edge Principle:** Clean, angled terminations, subtle chamfer accents, and hairline borders (0.5px to 1px) mimicking honed steel edges.
- **Controlled Incandescence:** Luminous glows (crimson, fiery amber, steel cyan) are reserved strictly for vital action states, active duels, victory tiers, and interactive focus.
- **Tactical Friction:** Low idle visual noise elevated by energetic, lightning-fast micro-interactions that mirror the speed of a blade strike.

## Colors

The color system anchors deeply into absolute darkness to maximize contrast against high-energy chromatic bursts.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surface Neutrals:** 
  - Void Canvas: `#0B0F17` (Deep carbon-black with a trace of deep cold slate)
  - Surface Substrate: `#111827` (Dark slate navy for structural chassis and nested trays)
  - Elevated Glass: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` with hairline borders tinted at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`
- **Primary — Crimson Strike (`#EF4444`):** Represents tournament stakes, lethal execution states, critical alerts, primary CTAs, and active bracket aggression.
- **Secondary — Steel Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** Embodies blade reflections, shields, technical brackets, telemetry, and secondary tactical interactions.
- **Tertiary — Fiery Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Denotes tournament champions, kill streaks, legendary ranks, and prestige badges.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - High Emphasis: `#F9FAFB`
  - Mid Emphasis: `#94A3B8`
  - Low / Disabled: `#475569`

### Color Role Rules
Glow shadows must always take the hex value of the underlying accent with an alpha range of `0.2` to `0.45` and a tight blur radius (never muddying global background surfaces).

## Typography

Typography balances monumental impact with razor-sharp analytical clarity.

### Family Roles
- **Headlines (`Montserrat`):** Used in heavy weights (700 to 900) for tournament headers, player callouts, match scores, and dramatic banners. Characterized by aggressive geometry, tight kerning, and raw velocity.
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Modern, hyper-clean neutral sans-serif designed for frictionless reading across dense match histories, rulebooks, weapon stats, and community forums.
- **Labels & Telemetry (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical, uppercase-biased monospace-inspired character forms used for timers, K/D ratios, seed numbers, arena coordinates, and interactive pill badges.

### Typographic Styling Guidelines
Headlines may feature all-caps styling with narrow tracking when introducing stages or duel states (e.g., `FINAL SHOWDOWN // ARENA 01`). Numerical statistics and bracket standings must prioritize tabular figures (`tnum`) to ensure stable visual alignment during live-updating scores.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for real-time tournament displays, modular bracket trees, and high-density player statistics.

### Grid & Breakpoint Specifications
- **Desktop (1280px and up):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Max-width content boundary is clamped at 1440px to retain cockpit-like visual tension.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) page margins. Sidebars collapse into glass slide-over sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) page margins. Horizontal scrolling bracket nodes and stacked duelist match cards.

### Spacing Cadence
Distances follow strict exponential jumps: micro-gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) bind stat counters and badge icons; structural intervals (`space-md`, `space-lg`, `space-xl`) define container breathing room and section segregation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered glass-and-steel paradigm rather than conventional fuzzy drop shadows.

### Depth Layers
- **Floor 0 (Canvas):** `#0B0F17` base layer accented with subtle, radial mesh gradients (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.05)` or `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04)`) anchored in the corners.
- **Floor 1 (Structural Trays / Brackets):** Solid `#111827` with a fine `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Floor 2 (Floating Match Cards / Modals):** Translucent acrylic composite with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background fill `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`, and a dual-border effect:
  - Outer: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Inner Top Highlight: A subtle gradient border simulating an overhead light striking polished steel (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0)`).
- **Floor 3 (Active State & Glow):** When a tournament card or duelist node becomes active, depth is communicated through a focused neon glow:
  - Box shadow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, soft-cornered industrial profile (`roundedness: 1`). Radii stay controlled at `0.25rem` (4px) for interactive controls and badges, reaching `0.5rem` (8px) on major structural tournament cards. 

### Geometric Rules
- Curvature is kept intentionally small and crisp to invoke the precision of blades, armor plating, and electronic scoring boxes. Pill shapes are forbidden for core containers, cards, and primary buttons; they are reserved exclusively for compact status pips and small telemetry tags.
- Buttons, badge plates, and match cards may incorporate a diagonal 45-degree chamfered edge (clip-path corner cut: 6px) on the top-right and bottom-left extremes for distinct esports branding moments.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Blade Crimson):** `#EF4444` solid background, text in white (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` typography in Space Grotesk. Features a linear-gradient top sheen (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` to transparent). Hover state expands a glowing red drop-shadow (`0 0 20px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.5)`) and lifts the element by `1px`.
- **Secondary CTA (Steel Cyan Ghost):** Translucent fill `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`, text in `#38BDF8`. Hover state fills the background with `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.16)` and adds a crisp steel-blue perimeter bloom.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Flat slate background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with `#94A3B8` label, transitioning to pure white on hover.

### Badges & Esports Chips
- Constructed with `label-sm` in Space Grotesk, uppercase, with letter spacing `0.1em`.
- **Live / In Combat:** `#EF4444` background at 15% opacity, `1px` border of `#EF4444`, text `#EF4444`, accompanied by a pulsing crimson indicator pip.
- **Victory / Champion:** Amber `#F59E0B` metallic treatment with high-sheen micro-borders and subtle gold ambient aura.

### Cards & Matchup Nodes
- Built on dark translucent glass (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`.
- Features razor metallic edges (`1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- **Versus (VS) Split Cards:** Split horizontally or vertically with an angled dividing slash. Winner side shifts into Cyan or Amber luminescence; eliminated side drops to 50% opacity and low-saturation slate.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- Inset dark wells (`rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.8)`) with `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Typography: `body-md`. Focus state triggers an immediate cyan perimeter light (`border-color: #38BDF8; box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Sharp, technical square/diamond geometry. Unchecked state features an empty dark slate box with a `1px` steel border. Checked state snaps to `#EF4444` or `#38BDF8` with an inner radiant glow and hard geometric glyph.

### Tournament-Specific Components
- **Bracket Node Connectors:** Hairline SVGs tinted in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Winning progression lines illuminate with neon crimson or cyan gradients trailing dynamic dash animations.
- **Fighter Telemetry Bar:** Segmented health/stamina style rank meters with sharp vertical separators and angled terminus edges.
